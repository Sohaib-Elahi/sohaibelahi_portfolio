# Visual system

Authority: AGENTS.md plus DESIGN-BRIEF.md, with creative decisions delegated by Sohaib in session.

Experience mode. The mathematical form is the main artifact; supporting interface stays quiet. Hero butterfly, gallery orbit, services bloom and about waves share one glyph renderer and pointer-space deformation. Work remains images only.

Dark default: red-tinted void surface, five-depth red structure, paper for readable body text. Light: warm paper, void structure, restrained brand red. Geist Pixel headings at 28px+, Geist Sans body at 16px+. Zero/12px radii. No decorative gradients, particle simulations, traced bitmaps, icon cards, pills or repeated fade-up entrances.

Two rails, opposite directions. Persistent minimal header. Clean portrait. Footer contains the real name, email and verified social link. Optional sound is off by default. Native focus, text selection and reduced motion remain usable.
