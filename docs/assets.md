## Animated ASCII portrait
Original: `raw/portrait/MeASCII.gif` — 912 × 1064, 72 frames at 25 fps, 2.88 seconds, 15,776,405 bytes. Source remains unchanged.

Delivery: `public/images/portrait-ascii.mp4` — 720 × 840, H.264/yuv420p, same frame rate and duration, no audio, fast-start metadata, 379,885 bytes (97.6% smaller). `public/images/portrait-ascii.webp` is the 54,692-byte first-frame poster. The original GIF is not copied into the production build.

Reproduce with `python scripts/prepare-portrait.py --ffmpeg /path/to/ffmpeg` (Pillow required). The component defers the MP4 source until near the viewport and only plays while visible. It pauses offscreen/in hidden tabs, loops without visible playback controls, and shows the poster without requesting video for reduced motion.

Comparison measured in Chrome on a local test server with cache disabled, 4 Mbps download throughput and 100 ms latency. These are complete asset-transfer times, not full-page load or first-frame times:

| Candidate | Bytes | Full transfer |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Original GIF | 15,776,405 | 31.694 s |
| 912px H.264 | 982,918 | 2.079 s |
| 720px H.264 — selected | 379,885 | 0.868 s |
| 720px VP9 | 354,911 | 0.820 s |

720px H.264 retains the red ASCII detail well at the portrait’s maximum 470px display width. VP9 saved only another 25 KB, so the site ships one broadly compatible video rather than redundant formats.
