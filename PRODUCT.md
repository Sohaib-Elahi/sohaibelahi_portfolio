# Product
<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->
## Platform
web
## Stack
Vite 6, React 19, strict TypeScript, Tailwind 4, GSAP/ScrollTrigger and Lenis, as specified.
## Users
Prospective design clients evaluating Sohaib’s brand, web and ecommerce work.
## Product Purpose
The site itself demonstrates Sohaib’s design and implementation ability through an original mathematical ASCII system, then makes the supplied work easy to inspect and contact easy to reach.
## Confirmed session decisions
Display name Sohaib; default dark; email contact; optional sound off by default; all 24 unique gallery images, original proportions. Creative direction delegated to the agent after review of the Figma-derived proposal.
## Constraints
One canvas and one ticker. No particles or traced imagery. Two prescribed themes and two font families. Images-only gallery. No invented clients, metrics or résumé facts. Respect reduced motion and keyboard access. Performance targets are gates, not claims.
## Open facts
Email and social destinations must be verified from supplied résumé. Final hero headline remains a user selection. Physical Android measurements require device access.
